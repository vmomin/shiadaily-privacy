ShiaDaily Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 6, 2026
Contact: shiadaily@icloud.com

This Privacy Policy explains how ShiaDaily (“ShiaDaily,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the ShiaDaily iOS app, Android app, Apple Watch features, widgets, and related services.

Summary

ShiaDaily provides prayer times, Islamic calendar information, Quran content, duas, ziyarats, audio, Qibla tools, tasbih, reminders, and related features.

The app does not require you to create a ShiaDaily account. We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for advertising profiling.

Some features are optional and may use your location, internet services, push notifications, cloud synchronization, Google Home, or in-app purchases.

Location Information

If you grant permission or manually provide a location, ShiaDaily may process approximate or precise location information.

Location is used for:

  • calculating prayer times;
  • determining Qibla direction;
  • identifying or displaying a city;
  • optional weather information; and
  • location-based reminders or widgets that you choose to use.

Location permission is optional. You may deny or revoke location access through your device settings. Where supported, you may manually select a city instead of granting device-location permission.

Prayer calculations and Qibla calculations are performed on the device using the selected coordinates.

For optional weather features, the app may send selected latitude and longitude coordinates to weather providers, including Open-Meteo, over HTTPS. Those providers may process standard technical request information, such as IP address, device information, requested URL, and request time, according to their own privacy policies and terms.

ShiaDaily does not use location for advertising, cross-app tracking, profiling, or sale. We do not operate our own server that stores your location.

Content and Audio Network Requests

Some Quran recitations, duas, ziyarats, and other audio may be streamed or downloaded from the internet.

Depending on the audio source, requests may be sent to services such as EveryAyah, Cloudflare R2, and other hosting providers used to deliver audio files.

These services may receive standard technical information associated with an internet request, such as IP address, device information, user agent, requested file, and request time. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party hosting providers.

Where available, you may use bundled or downloaded content to reduce network requests.

Information Stored on Your Device

ShiaDaily stores information locally so that app features work. This may include prayer location and calculation settings, prayer-time adjustments, favorites, bookmarks, reading progress, Quran, dua and ziyarat preferences, tasbih and qadha-tracker data, reminders, notification preferences, calendar adjustments, custom occasions, display settings, language settings, widget preferences, and Google Home connection settings.

This information is generally stored on your device and is not automatically sent to ShiaDaily.

iCloud and Apple Watch

If you use the iOS app and enable iCloud-related features, selected preferences and progress may synchronize through Apple’s iCloud services. iCloud is operated by Apple and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and your Apple ID settings. ShiaDaily cannot access the contents of your private iCloud storage.

If you use Apple Watch features, limited information may be transferred between your iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple system services such as WatchConnectivity. This supports complications, reminders, and synchronized information between your own devices.

Android Backup and Device Transfer

On Android, selected local app settings may be included in Android system backup or device-to-device transfer according to your Android, Google account, and device settings. These backups are controlled by Android and Google, not by ShiaDaily.

Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, ShiaDaily may use Apple Push Notification service or OneSignal to deliver reminders and other notifications.

Push-notification providers may process:

  • a device or push-subscription identifier;
  • app installation information;
  • notification delivery information;
  • notification opens or clicks; and
  • basic technical and diagnostic information.

ShiaDaily does not send your prayer coordinates to OneSignal. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Optional Google Home Integration

If you choose to connect ShiaDaily to Google Home, the app uses Google Sign-In and Google Home services. This may involve your Google account email address, authentication information, connected-device information, scenes, rooms, and commands needed to operate the integration.

This feature is optional and is initiated by you. Google processes this information under its privacy policy and the permissions you grant. You can disconnect Google Home from within the app or through your Google account and Google Home settings.

Optional Purchases

The Android app may use Google Play Billing for optional purchases. Apple platforms may use Apple StoreKit for optional purchases.

Payment details are processed by Google Play or Apple. ShiaDaily does not receive or store your full payment-card details. ShiaDaily may receive purchase status, product information, transaction identifiers, and entitlement information needed to provide or restore a purchase.

Feedback

If you voluntarily submit feedback, ShiaDaily may send your feedback message, selected category, optional email address, app version, device model, operating-system version, and language or locale information.

Feedback is sent through Web3Forms and is used to review, respond to, and improve the app. Providing an email address is optional.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate app features;
  • calculate prayer times and Qibla direction;
  • display optional weather information;
  • save settings, favorites, and progress;
  • deliver notifications and reminders;
  • provide audio and other requested content;
  • support Apple Watch, widgets, and Google Home features;
  • process and restore optional purchases;
  • respond to feedback;
  • maintain security and reliability; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for advertising profiling or automated decisions about you.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

ShiaDaily may transmit information to third parties when necessary to provide an optional feature you request. These providers may include:

  • Apple services, including iCloud, StoreKit, Apple Push Notification service, and WatchConnectivity;
  • Google services, including Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In, Google Home, Android backup, and device transfer;
  • OneSignal for push-notification delivery;
  • Open-Meteo or other weather providers;
  • audio and content-hosting providers such as EveryAyah and Cloudflare R2; and
  • Web3Forms for voluntarily submitted feedback.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not share your information with third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or respond to a valid legal request.

Data Retention

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or change the relevant settings.

iCloud and Android backup data are retained according to your Apple, Google, and device settings.

Third-party providers may retain technical request logs, notification records, purchase records, or feedback according to their own retention policies.

ShiaDaily does not maintain a user-account database or a central profile containing your prayer location.

Security

Network requests made by ShiaDaily use HTTPS/TLS where supported by the relevant service. Local data is protected using the storage and security controls provided by iOS, Android, Apple, and Google.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children

ShiaDaily is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that requires parental consent under applicable law.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information improperly, contact us at shiadaily@icloud.com.

Your Choices

  • Deny or revoke location permission.
  • Manually select a city where supported.
  • Disable notifications.
  • Disable iCloud synchronization.
  • Disable Android backup through your device or Google settings.
  • Disconnect Google Home.
  • Avoid optional audio streaming.
  • Decline to submit feedback or provide an email address.
  • Delete the app or clear its data.

You may contact us at shiadaily@icloud.com with questions or to request deletion of information that ShiaDaily controls. Information held by Apple, Google, OneSignal, Open-Meteo, audio hosts, or Web3Forms must be managed through those providers directly.

International Users

Depending on the services you use, information may be processed in countries outside your own. Apple, Google, OneSignal, Open-Meteo, content-hosting providers, and Web3Forms may process information according to their own policies and applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when updates are made. Continued use of the app after an update means that you acknowledge the revised policy.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or concerns may be sent to shiadaily@icloud.com.
